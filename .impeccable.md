# Impeccable Design Context

This document defines the design principles and context for the Image to PDF project, ensuring all AI-generated UI remains consistent with the `impeccable.style` framework.

## Design Context

### Users
Professionals handling sensitive documents who require a high degree of privacy and security. Their primary goal is to convert images to PDF without any data leaving their local machine.

### Brand Personality
**Secure, Private, Professional.**
The interface must evoke trust through technical competence and visual restraint. It is a tool for a specific job, emphasizing data sovereignty (100% client-side) and professional output.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Stitch Framework (Warm Minimalism).**
A refined, editorial aesthetic that moves away from sterile tech-white toward warm, professional tones.
- **Color System:** OKLCH-based (Warm Paper backgrounds, Deep Charcoal text, Refined Blue accents).
- **Typography:** High-contrast pairing of 'Instrument Serif' (headings) and 'Geist' or clean sans-serif (body).
- **Layout:** "No-Card" policy—use whitespace, subtle 1px dividers, and intentional asymmetry instead of heavy containers.

### Design Principles
1. **Precision Typography:** Use font weight, scale, and high-quality typefaces as the primary design element.
2. **Warmth & Trust:** Employ subtle, warm off-white tones (`oklch(98% 0.01 20)`) to feel more human and professional than pure white.
3. **Negative Space as a Tool:** Whitespace is active and rhythmic, directing focus to the security of the local processing.
4. **Structural Clarity:** Replace traditional cards with clean list-based views and subtle dividers to maintain a lightweight, fast feel.
5. **Privacy-Centric Feedback:** Micro-interactions should feel organic and reassuring, confirming the safe, 100% client-side lifecycle of the user's data.
